Pokémon Binder Tracker is an unofficial fan utility, unaffiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK, or The Pokémon Company.
Pokémon names, characters, card artwork, and sprites belong to their respective owners.
Species metadata: PokéAPI (https://pokeapi.co/). Card metadata: TCGdex (https://tcgdex.dev/), MIT licensed.
Species sprites: PokeAPI/sprites (https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites). See the accompanying license notices.
Card coverage reflects the pinned catalog snapshot and may be incomplete. National Dex ownership is tracked by species. Set binders track card numbers and printings.
TCGplayer Product ID crosswalk: matched using public catalog metadata supplied by TCGCSV (https://tcgcsv.com/).
Market estimates: Cardmarket public Pokémon price guide (https://www.cardmarket.com/en/Pokemon/Data/Price-Guide) converted with Bank of Canada exchange rates (https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/).
